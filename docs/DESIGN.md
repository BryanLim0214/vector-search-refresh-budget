# Design & Specification Traceability (`proposal/proposal.tex` $\rightarrow$ `dvs/`)

Every technical requirement in `proposal/proposal.tex` is mapped directly to its implementation in `dvs/`.

## 1. Problem Description and Method (Proposal Section 4, Paragraph 1)

| Proposal Sentence | Implementation |
|---|---|
| *"Each passage and query is a 384-float unit vector (1,536 bytes) with dot-product similarity."* | `dvs/bus.py`: `DIM = 384`, `FLOAT_BYTES = 4`, `VECTOR_BYTES = 1536`. `dvs/data.py`: `normalize_l2()` ensures unit-length `float32` vectors; `dvs/shards.py` uses `hnswlib.Index(space="ip", dim=384)`; `dvs/metrics.py` uses `faiss.IndexFlatIP(384)`. |
| *"Initial passages are grouped into $S$ shards by $k$-means; new passages go to the shard with the nearest initial center (no data movement)."* | `dvs/shards.py`: `build_initial_shards()` clusters the 50,000 initial vectors into $S$ shards using `faiss.Kmeans(..., spherical=True)`; `ShardRouterInitializer.assign_shards()` routes both initial and newly arrived vectors to the nearest initial centroid without moving existing vectors. |
| *"Each shard summarizes its passages with $c = 16$ centers (24,576 bytes)."* | `dvs/bus.py`: `SUMMARY_CENTERS = 16`, `SUMMARY_BYTES = 24576`. `dvs/summary.py`: `ShardSummary` stores `centers` of shape `(16, 384)` (`float32`). |
| *"The coordinator holds each shard's last sent (published) summary, scores shards by their closest published center to the query, searches the top $m$ shards, and merges candidates into 10 results."* | `dvs/coordinator.py`: `Coordinator.published_summaries` stores each shard's last published `ShardSummary`; `ShardSummary.query_scores()` computes $\max_{c} \langle q, \mu_{s,c} \rangle$; `Coordinator.route_queries()` selects the top $m$ shards; `Coordinator.merge_candidates()` deduplicates and merges shard results into the top 10. |
| *"Each epoch, a shard builds a candidate summary by 16-center $k$-means on its current vectors, starting from its published centers, sent whole when refreshed."* | `dvs/shards.py`: `Shard.update_candidate_summary()` calls `compute_summary_kmeans(..., warm_start_centers=self.published_summary.centers)`; `Coordinator.receive_summary()` publishes the candidate summary whole and logs `24,576` bytes on `ByteBus`. |

## 2. Clock, Budget, and Missed Neighbors (Proposal Section 4, Paragraph 2)

| Proposal Sentence | Implementation |
|---|---|
| *"Time advances in epochs of fixed insertions and queries, not seconds."* | `dvs/streams.py`: `build_epoch_schedule()` emits discrete `EpochBatch` objects; `dvs/run.py`: `run_single_simulation()` steps through `epoch = 0 .. num_epochs - 1`. |
| *"Planned defaults (fixed in a pilot) are $S = 32$ shards (sweeping 16, 32, 64), $c = 16$, $m \in \{2, 4\}$, 1,000 insertions and $Q = 200$ queries per epoch (sweeping $Q$), recent window $W = 2$ epochs, and budget $B \in \{2, 4, 8, 16\}$ summaries per epoch (49,152 to 393,216 bytes) so $B$ binds."* | `configs/prereg.yaml`, `configs/exp1_steady.yaml`, `configs/exp2_cluster_order.yaml`, `configs/exp3_query_shift.yaml`, `configs/exp4_sweeps.yaml`. |
| *"A query's missed neighbors is the count of true top-10 neighbors on skipped shards."* | `dvs/metrics.py`: `evaluate_epoch_queries()` counts for each query how many of its exact Faiss top-10 neighbors reside on shards where `searched_shards_mask[q_idx, shard_id]` is `False` (and not locally pushed to the coordinator). |

## 3. Refresh Score and Overhead (Proposal Section 4, Paragraph 3)

| Proposal Sentence | Implementation |
|---|---|
| *"On $Q_s = 64$ sample queries kept apart from test queries, the coordinator searches each sample query on its top $m$ shards, which return only their 10 best scores ($64 \times m \times 40$ bytes), then sends each shard the $m$-th shard score and 10th-best result score per query (512 bytes per shard)."* | `dvs/policies.py`: `PolicyController.compute_recovered_neighbor_raw_scores()` routes the 64 sample queries to their top $m$ shards, calls `shard.search_batch_scores_only()` to obtain top-10 scores, computes `mth_shard_scores` and `tenth_best_scores`, and `dvs/bus.py`: `log_recovered_neighbor_overhead()` logs `64 * m * 40` bytes (`rn_sample_search_reply`) and `512 * S` bytes (`rn_cutoffs`). |
| *"A flip is a change in whether a shard is among the top $m$ for a query. Comparing published and candidate summaries, a shard scores only flips lifting it into the top $m$, weighted by how many of its new vectors beat that query's 10th-best score, plus 0.5 per epoch waited, after each rule's scores are scaled to $[0, 1]$ across shards."* | `dvs/policies.py`: `compute_recovered_neighbor_raw_scores()` identifies lift-in flips (`(~in_top_m_pub) & beats_mth`), weights each lift-in flip by `np.sum((lifted_queries @ shard.new_vectors.T) > lifted_cutoffs, axis=1)`, scales raw scores to $[0, 1]$ via `scale_scores_unit_interval()`, and adds `0.5 * epochs_since_refresh`. |
| *"Drop-out flips are logged, not scored, as a shard cannot know if the replacement shard holds more true neighbors. Estimates assume other summaries stay fixed within an epoch."* | `dvs/policies.py`: `dropout_flips` (`in_top_m_pub & falls_below`) are counted per shard and recorded in `epochs.parquet` (`total_dropout_flips`) and `summary.json`, without contributing to `raw_scores`. |
| *"Overhead counts toward $B$ each epoch: $512 \times S$ bytes of cutoffs, $4 \times S$ bytes of shard scores, and the sample searches (21,632 bytes at $S = 32$, $m = 2$). The sample vectors (98,304 bytes per shard, sent once at setup) are reported both ways: as a separate setup cost, and spread over the 50 epochs and charged to $B$."* | `dvs/bus.py`: `recovered_neighbor_overhead_bytes()` equals `21,632` at $S=32, m=2$; deducted from `remaining_budget` in `PolicyController.step_epoch()`. Setup sample vectors (`98,304 * S` bytes) are logged at setup (`setup_sample_vectors_bytes`), reported amortized in `budget_bytes_with_amortized_setup`, and optionally deducted directly from per-epoch $B$ when `charge_setup_to_budget=True`. |

## 4. Comparisons, Alternatives, and Bounds (Proposal Section 4, Paragraph 4)

| Proposal Sentence | Implementation |
|---|---|
| *"Under budget $B$, I compare five refresh rules, each sending whole summaries (24,576 bytes): round-robin; data-change score (new vectors / shard size $\times$ mean distance to nearest published center); recovered-neighbor score; and splitting the budget by query popularity or by shard size, the QL and SS policies of [shokouhi2007updating]."* | `dvs/policies.py`: `PolicyController` implements `round_robin`, `data_change`, `recovered_neighbor`, `ql` (using `coordinator.past_top_m_counts`), and `ss` (using `shard.size`). |
| *"Two alternatives get equal bytes: extra shard searches (1,656 bytes each: a 1,536-byte query out, ten 8-byte IDs and 4-byte scores back) and pushing new vectors (1,536 bytes each, searched at the coordinator); extra searches go to the queries whose $m$-th and $(m+1)$-th shard scores are closest, and the newest vectors are pushed first."* | `dvs/policies.py`: `plan_extra_searches()` ranks candidate extra searches by smallest `ranked_scores[q, m-1] - ranked_scores[q, m]` up to `B // 1656`; `push_vectors` pops up to `B // 1536` newest vectors from `_unpushed_stream_buffer` and calls `coordinator.receive_pushed_vectors()`. |
| *"Two bounds are always-fresh summaries and searching all $S$ shards."* | `dvs/policies.py` and `dvs/run.py`: `always_fresh` refreshes all $S$ summaries each epoch; `all_shards` sets `m_routed = S`. |

## 5. Data, Streams, and Evaluation Metrics (Proposal Section 5)

| Proposal Sentence | Implementation |
|---|---|
| *"I will embed 100,000 MS MARCO passages and queries into 384-dimensional unit vectors with the `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` model... 50,000 initial and 50,000 streamed passages, 1,000 MS MARCO development-set test queries (each epoch draws its $Q$ queries from them), and 64 sample queries... (hash sharding by ID vs. $k$-means is a reference check)."* | `dvs/data.py`: `build_dataset()` embeds 100,000 passages from `BeIR/msmarco`, 1,000 dev queries (`dev.tsv`), and 64 disjoint train queries (`train.tsv`). `dvs/shards.py`: `ShardRouterInitializer` supports `sharding_mode="kmeans"` and `"hash"`. |
| *"Each shard searches with `hnswlib` (10 candidates), and Faiss gives exact top-10 ground truth."* | `dvs/shards.py`: `Shard.search()` queries `hnswlib.Index(space="ip", dim=384)` for $k=10$; `dvs/metrics.py`: `GroundTruthIndex` queries `faiss.IndexFlatIP(384)` for $k=10$. |
| *"I will test steady arrivals, the Big-ANN streaming benchmark's cluster-order arrivals, and query shift away from the sample queries (plus bursts if time allows)."* | `dvs/streams.py`: `order_stream_passages()` (`steady` vs 64-cluster `cluster_order`), `compute_query_shift_pool()` (lowest mean dot-product similarity to the 64 sample queries), and `compute_epoch_insert_counts(..., bursts=True)`. |
| *"Each epoch I record overall recall@10, recent recall@10 (fraction of true top-10 neighbors from the last $W = 2$ epochs returned; queries with none are left out and counted), missed neighbors, bytes, CPU time, rank correlation between estimated and true recovered neighbors, and the break-even query rate $Q$ between refresh and wider search. Runs use 5 seeds and 95% intervals from a paired bootstrap over test queries..."* | `dvs/metrics.py` (`evaluate_epoch_queries`, `CPUTimer`, `compute_spearman_correlation`, `paired_bootstrap_ci`) and `dvs/run.py` (`run_single_simulation`, `run_from_config`). |
