# Result Notes from `audit/SKEPTIC_MAIN.md` and `audit/SKEPTIC_M3.md`

All result numbers in the paper are generated by `paper/scripts/gen_numbers.py` into `paper/generated/numbers.tex`.

## 1. Claim 1: H1 PASS: Under centroid placement, fresh summaries add little (`+0.55` pp steady, `+2.29` pp `new_topic`)

- **Skeptic Verdict:** `HOLDS WITH CAVEAT.`
- **Key Caveats:**
  - Both upper 95% CI bounds (`+1.12` and `+3.17` pp) are below `5` pp, so the result holds on the interval as well as the point estimate.
  - "Little" is relative to large routing headroom (`oracle_routing - never_refresh` is `+9.70` pp on `steady` and `+16.92` pp on `new_topic`, so fresh summaries close only `5.6%` and `13.5%` of the gap).
  - Cause is structural: under `nearest_initial`, new vectors go to the shard with the nearest initial center.
  - H1 restates a pilot observation and reuses seed `42` as one of the five main seeds (`42..46`), so it is a confirmation rather than an independent test.

## 2. Claim 2: H2 and H2b PASS: At equal bytes, `extra_search`, `extra_search_online`, and `push_vectors` beat the best refresh rule

- **Skeptic Verdict:** `HOLDS WITH CAVEAT.`
- **Key Caveats:**
  1. `push_vectors` is a sampling effect, not a routing improvement: its recent-recall gain over `never_refresh` equals `(share of new vectors pushed) x (share of recent neighbors routing misses)` to within `0.1` to `1.2` pp across conditions. On overall recall it gains only `+0.54` pp (`steady`) and `+0.32` pp (`new_topic`) over the best refresh rule, and it relies on uncharged coordinator memory (up to `3,150` vectors at `B = 4`) and per-query exact scans.
  2. `extra_search` is a generic routing improvement rather than a freshness method: its overall-recall gain over the best refresh rule (`+3.03` pp `steady`, `+2.25` pp `new_topic`) is as large as its recent-recall gain. Both `extra_search` and `push_vectors` beat every refresh rule in every seed and also beat unbudgeted `always_fresh`.
  3. Equal bytes is not equal work: `extra_search` adds up to `59` shard searches per epoch and an extra round trip; `push_vectors` adds coordinator storage and per-query scans; refresh rules spend shard CPU on k-means and receive uncharged protocol favors (`recovered_neighbor` setup broadcast `3,145,728` B, `data_change` score messages `128` B/epoch, and the `has_new_vectors` bit).
  4. Causal `extra_search_online` loses only `0.21` pp (`steady`) and `0.13` pp (`new_topic`) vs. batch `extra_search` at `Q = 200`, showing batch look-ahead does not drive H2.
  5. Break-even: on `new_topic`, `extra_search` and `extra_search_online` fall behind `data_change` at `Q = 800` (`-1.01` and `-1.06` pp), while `push_vectors` stays ahead at all `Q`. At `Q = 50`, batch `extra_search` spends `9` of `59` searches on the `(m+2)`-th shard while `extra_search_online` leaves at least `14,904` B/epoch unspent.

## 3. Claim 3: M2 (`time_segment`): All refresh rules tie `always_fresh`; `push_vectors` vs. refresh across `B`

- **Skeptic Verdict:**
  - On the ties: `HOLDS (by construction).`
  - On `push_vectors` vs. refresh across `B`: on `new_topic`, fresh summaries beat `push_vectors` at every `B` (`+19.40`, `+16.04`, and `+9.64` pp at `B = 2, 4, 8`), and `push_vectors` also loses on overall recall (`-8.97`, `-8.04`, and `-6.12` pp). On `steady`, `push_vectors` wins at every `B` (`-1.40`, `-4.31`, and `-10.15` pp for refresh minus push).
- **Key Caveats:**
  - Every refresh rule equals `always_fresh` on every query (`PRED_M2_TIES` max abs per-query diff `0.0`), so M2 cannot rank refresh rules, `H3` is identically zero, `H4`'s "best refresh rule" is `always_fresh`, and `sens` (`A = 8`) reproduces M2 for seeds `42..44` and carries no information about the cap.
  - On M2 `steady`, `never_refresh` recent recall is `0.0627` (chance level `2/32 = 0.0625`), and fresh summaries raise it only to `0.0793` (`+1.66` pp, failing `GATE_M2`) while lowering overall recall@10 by `-1.59` pp (`[-1.74, -1.45]`, negative in all 5 seeds).

## 4. Claim 4: M3 (`time_segment_g`, `G = 8`): Active shards, `GATE_M3`, `H3b`, `PRED_M3_B8_TIES`, `H4b`, and Spearman

- **Active shards (`audit/M3_DESIGN_CHECK.md`):** New vectors reached all 8 active shards in `250/250` steady epochs (`min = 8`) and `244/250` new-topic epochs (`mean = 7.96`, `min = 5`, with 2 epochs at 5 shards and 4 epochs at 7 shards), or `494/500` total epochs.
- **`GATE_M3`:** Fails on `steady` (`+1.16` pp `[+0.38, +1.91]`, lowering overall recall@10 by `-0.91` pp `[-1.02, -0.79]`) and passes on `new_topic` (`+6.74` pp `[+5.16, +8.16]`, overall `+1.73` pp, though seed 44 is `+3.69` pp).
- **`H3b` (`B = 4`, `new_topic`):** Fails. `recovered_neighbor` loses to `data_change` by `-3.60` pp `[-4.93, -2.26]`, ties `ql` (`+0.50` pp) and `ss` (`-0.48` pp), and beats `round_robin` (`+2.38` pp). Probing costs `21,632` B/epoch (`22.0%` of `B = 4`, leaving 3 summaries vs. 4, though charging `data_change`'s `128` B/epoch score messages would also leave 3 summaries), and `recovered_neighbor` at `B = 8` (7 summaries, `0.3003`) still trails `data_change` at `B = 4` (4 summaries, `0.3135`).
- **`H4b`:** On `steady`, `push_vectors` wins at every `B` (`-1.79`, `-3.67`, `-7.17` pp). On `new_topic`, `data_change` beats `extra_search_online` (`+2.75` pp) and `push_vectors` (`+1.47` pp `[+0.03, +2.90]`, fragile) at `B = 2`, ties `push_vectors` at `B = 4` (`+1.04` pp `[-0.50, +2.53]`), and loses to `push_vectors` at `B = 8` (`-3.59` pp `[-5.37, -1.86]`).
- **Spearman rank correlation:** In every M1 and M3 condition, `recovered_neighbor`'s 64-query estimator correlates less strongly with true recovered neighbors than `N_new(s)` (`0.03` vs `0.20` M1 steady, `0.18` vs `0.50` M1 new-topic, `0.10` vs `0.47` M3 steady, `0.26` vs `0.63` M3 new-topic at `B = 4, Q = 200`), and its raw score is zero for all 32 shards in `167/250` (`66.8%`), `90/250` (`36.0%`), `121/250` (`48.4%`), and `62/250` (`24.8%`) epochs of those four conditions.
