About 50 seconds Third case: eight shards share each time step’s new passages. This is the hardest case, and here the size of the budget decides. The dashed line is never refreshing. On new topics, compare the teal and orange bars. With the smallest budget, refreshing is ahead of push by about one and a half points. But that lead is fragile. The most-changed rule needs each shard to send a tiny four-byte score, and in our main runs those bytes were not charged to the budget. The budget buys whole summaries, so when we charged for those scores, the rule could afford only one summary instead of two, and the lead disappeared. With a budget of four there is no clear winner, and with a budget of eight push wins by about three and a half points. On mixed topics, push wins at every budget for finding new matches. If you count older matches too, live extra search does even better than push.
