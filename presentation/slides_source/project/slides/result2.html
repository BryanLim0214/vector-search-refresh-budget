About 45 seconds Second case: all of a time step’s new passages go to a single shard. Now the old summaries fail, and the kind of topic decides what helps. On the left, new topics. When the new passages share a new topic, one fresh summary describes them well. Refreshing wins by a lot, about twenty-two and a half points over never refreshing, and it beats push at every budget. On the right, mixed topics. When the new passages mix all topics, sixteen summary points cannot describe them. Refreshing gains less than two points, it slightly lowers recall on older matches, and push wins at every budget. One more detail: here all five refresh rules tie, because they all refresh the one shard that changed. A code review caught that before we ran anything, and it is why we added the eight-shard setting on the next slide.
