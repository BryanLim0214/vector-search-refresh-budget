About 30 seconds Here is the main finding in one sentence: where new data lands decides how to spend the network budget. When each new passage goes to the shard with the nearest starting center, extra search and push both beat every refresh rule. When one shard takes all of a step’s new passages, fresh summaries win on new topics and push wins on mixed topics. When eight shards share them, push wins on mixed topics; on new topics, refresh has only a fragile lead at the smallest budget, there is no clear winner at the middle one, and push wins at the largest.
